## quasar-select-loading-undefined
tags: quasar, q-select, loading, spinner, undefined, boolean, vue
symptom: A q-select shows a spinner forever.
trap: Binding :loading to a value that is undefined instead of a real boolean leaves the loading animation running.
fix: cast the bound value (:loading="!!isFetching") and initialise the flag to false.
verified: reported from production use, Quasar 2.x, 2026-11
versions: Quasar 2.x
