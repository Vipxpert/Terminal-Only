# /llm_harness/playbooks/review.txt
# use when: before saying a piece of work or a project is complete or done, at the end of a milestone or a session, and whenever the owner asks what is left or what was missed
Review before calling anything done. Read first, then report.
1. Read docs/backlog.md, the Unreleased part of CHANGELOG.md, the Todo notes (@NOTE_LIST), stale verdicts (see @HELP VERDICTS), open debug ledgers and the pinned goal.
2. Check every claim of "done" against evidence: which test ran, on which OS, on real data or a sample. Anything built but never proven where it matters is unverified.
3. Look for what nobody wrote down: questions waiting on someone, findings mentioned in a chat but never fixed, rules the code does not enforce, documentation that no longer matches the code. Then look back at the work itself for friction: the same workaround, refusal or manual step more than once, or anything the owner had to remind you of.
4. Add every new item to docs/backlog.md (kind, what, why it matters, date) in this same script; remove items that are really finished.
5. Report at most 7 items, the most valuable or riskiest first, each with one line of why it matters to the pinned goal, its evidence (or the word speculative when there is none) and the smallest next step, and say plainly which ones need the owner (a decision, a test on their machine, an account). Propose a new capability only from evidence, and send a big one into @PLAYBOOK plan-first or plan-project instead of starting it.
6. Never call the work complete while unverified items remain: say "complete except: ..." instead.
